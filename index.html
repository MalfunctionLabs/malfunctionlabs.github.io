About // UserSubmit

Build it.
Break it.
Learn why.

Industrial mechanic, systems builder, modder, designer and habitual asker of: “Why the fuck are we doing it that way?”

Malfunction Labs did not begin with a pitch deck. It grew out of years spent around machines, production floors, maintenance problems, workflows, games, mods and people doing complicated work with systems that could have been simpler.

The common thread is practical: look at what is actually happening, fix the thing that is actually wrong, and leave the system better than you found it. That mindset came from manufacturing and maintenance long before it ever touched code. Centerlines, tracking, preventive maintenance, process improvement and troubleshooting all teach the same lesson: the machine does not care what the meeting said it should do. It only cares what the system actually does.

The Lab applies that thinking everywhere. A mod can become a systems-design problem. A maintenance headache can become software. A simulator can become research. A ridiculous conversation can become a company operating principle if it keeps proving useful under pressure.

Trade DNAIndustrial maintenance + manufacturing

AI is part of the toolchain here, but not the excuse. Specialists can research, organize, prototype, test, model, route, document and argue with each other. UserSubmit remains the final runtime authority. Access is not authority, approval is not a trigger, and a build passing is not the same thing as the thing actually working.

The Operating Philosophy

We do not need every idea to work. We need to test it honestly, learn something useful, preserve what mattered, adjust the design, and move forward.
